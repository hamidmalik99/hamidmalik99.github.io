Questions fractional cfos ask

What should a fractional CFO website include?

A clear explanation of what a fractional CFO does, who it's for, how engagements work, your experience, and a simple way to book a call. Many firms also explain typical scope or price ranges so owners can tell quickly whether you fit.

Should we show our prices?

You don't have to list exact fees, but explaining how you charge (monthly retainer, days per month, project fees) reduces calls from poor-fit leads. I can help you word it either way.

Have you built websites for finance firms before?

Yes. My recent work includes the Longfield Financial Advisory site in the UK, including its Fractional CFO, Financial Modelling and Business Valuation pages, built from approved mockups in Elementor.

Can you help with SEO for a finance firm?

Yes. I handle technical and on-page SEO and can set up location pages and an articles section. I'll explain which searches are realistic to target for your area.
