Questions home care agencies ask

Do home care agencies have to show their CQC rating on their website?

Yes. Under Regulation 20A, CQC-rated providers in England must display their latest rating clearly and prominently on their website. CQC provides an official widget, and I add it so it updates automatically. Check CQC's current guidance for your service.

What do families look for on a home care website?

The areas you cover, the types of care you provide, your CQC rating, how to contact you quickly, and a sense of who your carers are. Clear answers to those make a call much more likely.

Can the website help us recruit carers?

Yes. A dedicated jobs section with a short application form that works well on phones, plus job details search engines can read, usually brings in more applicants than a generic contact form.

Can our office staff update the site?

Yes. I build it in WordPress with simple editing for vacancies, news and team details, and show your team how to use it.
