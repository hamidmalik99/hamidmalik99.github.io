Questions engineering firms ask

What should a structural engineer's website include?

Clear service pages, a project portfolio, your registrations and insurances, the areas you cover, and an enquiry form that asks for the site address, project type and any drawings. Those let a builder or homeowner decide quickly and give you enough to quote.

Can you work with the photos and project list we already have?

Yes. I can organise existing photos, case notes and capability statements into a project portfolio, and tell you where a few extra photos or short descriptions would help.

Will the site help us rank in our local area?

I set up the technical and on-page SEO, including service-area pages and your Google Business Profile details. Rankings also depend on competition and reviews, so I explain what to expect for your area rather than promise positions.

How long does an engineering firm website take?

A typical 6 to 10 page site takes 2 to 4 weeks once content is ready. You get a fixed quote and a timeline in writing before work starts.
