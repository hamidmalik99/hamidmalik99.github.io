The problem

Where most industrial supplier websites fall short

Buyers can't find the part

Customers search by part number, load rating or size. A catalogue without good filters and search sends them to a competitor.

Not everything is buy-now

Many products need a quote, a site survey or trade pricing. The site has to handle quotes as well as checkout.

Product data is messy

Spec sheets, certificates and manuals live in different places. Putting them on each product page saves your sales team calls.

Questions industrial suppliers ask

Can WooCommerce handle quote requests as well as online orders?

Yes. Products can be set to buy online, request a quote, or both. Quote requests collect quantities and details and arrive with your team as an enquiry or a draft order.

Can trade customers see their own prices?

Yes. Trade accounts can see customer-specific or tiered pricing once logged in, while the public sees list prices or a quote button.

We have thousands of products in spreadsheets. Can you import them?

Yes. I import products in bulk from spreadsheets or supplier feeds, including attributes for filtering and attached documents such as spec sheets.

Have you built sites for industrial companies before?

Yes. My portfolio includes sites for lifting, tooling and industrial supply companies in the UK and Australia. You can see them on this page and on my work page.
