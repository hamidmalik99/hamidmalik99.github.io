Questions business brokers ask

How can a business broker get seller leads from their own website?

Owners search for things like “sell my business” plus their city, or how to value a business. Pages that answer those questions, a confidential enquiry form and a clear explanation of your process give them a reason to contact you directly.

Can listings be confidential?

Yes. Listings can show a teaser (sector, region, revenue band) with full details released only after a buyer signs an NDA through the site.

Have you worked with firms in this space?

Yes. I built the website for Longfield Business Group, a UK firm that acquires owner-managed service businesses, and its sister site Longfield Financial Advisory, which includes buying, selling and valuation pages.

Can I add and remove listings myself?

Yes. I build listings as simple WordPress entries with fields for each detail, so you can add, update or mark them sold without touching code.
